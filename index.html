Performance Training

Goal: Build total body strength, focusing on power transfer from the ground up.

Frequency: 3-4 days/week

Freshmen / JV Focus

Master form with bodyweight or light resistance. Do not push heavy weights until mechanics are perfect.

Varsity Focus (Mass & Power)

Progressive overload. During Phases 2 & 3, focus on hypertrophy (8-12 reps) if you need to add mass, then transition to lower rep power sets.

Muscle Gain Protocol (Hypertrophy Block)

For players needing to add physical mass (especially Varsity): During the first 6-8 weeks of lifting, utilize a hypertrophy rep range on major compound lifts. This means 3 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions, leaving 1-2 reps in the tank (RPE 8). Combine this with the 'Game Plan: Growth' in the Nutrition tab.

Arm Care & Maintenance

Protecting the arm is the most critical aspect of the off-season. This routine must be done before EVERY lifting or throwing session.

Rotator Cuff Health

External & Internal Rotation

External Rotation (Bands/Light DBs): 2 sets of 15 reps.

Keep elbow tucked at side. Pull away from body.

Internal Rotation (Bands): 2 sets of 15 reps.

Keep elbow tucked. Pull inward across stomach.

Coach's Note: Pitchers often have strong internal rotation but weak external rotation. Focus on slow, controlled eccentric (lowering) movements on external rotation.

Scapular Stability

  • Band Pull-Aparts (2x15)
  • Face Pulls (2x15)
  • Scapular Push-ups (2x15)
  • Sleeper Stretches
  • Y/T/W/I Raises (1-3 lbs or no weight)

Band & Plyo Work (Phases 2 & 3)

Introduce these carefully to build arm speed and dynamic stability.

Plyo Balls (Weighted Baseballs)

Freshmen/JV: Focus strictly on mechanics and constraint drills (pivot pick-offs, roll-ins) using lighter plyo balls. NO max velocity.
Varsity: Can incorporate heavier balls for warm-ups and drill work, always prioritizing form over speed.

Throwing Build-Up Progression

IMPORTANT: Never throw through sharp pain. Soreness is okay; pain is not.
1

Weeks 1-4: Rest

NO THROWING

Focus entirely on physical recovery, mobility, and the beginning of the lifting program.

2

Weeks 5-8: Light Catch

  • Frequency: 2 days a week
  • Distance: 60-90 feet max
  • Intensity: 50%
  • Focus on mechanics, arm path, and fluid motion.
3

Weeks 9-12: Long Toss Ramp Up

  • Frequency: 3 days a week
  • Begin long toss program (90ft → 120ft → 150ft).
  • Incorporate "pull downs" on the way back in.
Freshmen: Cap distance at 120-150 feet. Varsity: Push to 200+ feet if arm strength allows.
4

Weeks 13-16: Mound Work

Transition from flat ground to the mound.

Wk 13: Flat ground bullpens (75%), FB command.
Wk 14: 1-2 light mound pens (20-25 pitches, FB only).
Wk 15: 2 pens. Intro changeups. 85% intensity (30-35 pitches).
Wk 16: 2 pens. Intro breaking balls (Varsity/approved JV). Simulate game situations (40-45 pitches).

Phase 1 (Wks 1-4): Rest

NO THROWING

Phase 2 (Wks 5-8): Light Catch

2x/week, 60-90ft max, 50% intensity.

Phase 3 (Wks 9-12): Long Toss

Transition to mound. Build pitch count (20 -> 45). Intro off-speed gradually.

Nutrition Guide

Fueling the body is essential for recovery, adding lean muscle mass, and increasing velocity on the mound.

Hydration

Half body weight in oz daily. Add electrolytes on training days.

Protein

0.8-1.2g per lb of body weight. Chicken, turkey, lean beef, fish, eggs, greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey protein, lentils.

Carbs

Primary energy. Sweet/white potatoes, brown/white rice, oatmeal, quinoa, whole-wheat pasta/bread, beans, bananas, berries.

Fats

Hormone & joint health. Avocados, mixed nuts, chia/flax seeds, olive oil, peanut butter, salmon, cheese.

Game Plan: Growth (Mass Gain)

Add Mass

Essential for increasing velocity. Mass = Gas.

  • Must be in a caloric surplus (+300-500 calories above maintenance daily).
  • High Protein: Aim for 1.0 - 1.2g of protein per lb of body weight to build muscle.
  • Add a high-calorie protein shake (milk, oats, peanut butter, whey) daily.
  • Eat frequently: 3 large main meals + 2-3 substantial snacks. Never skip breakfast.

Game Plan: Maintenance

Lean Out
  • Focus on lean proteins and high vegetable intake.
  • Limit processed sugars and refined carbohydrates.
  • Maintain a slight caloric deficit or eat at maintenance level.

Workout Fuel Timing

Pre-Workout / Throwing

Carb-heavy snack 1-2 hours before.

Example: Banana, oatmeal, toast with jam.

Post-Workout Recovery

Protein & carb mix within 30-45 minutes.

Example: Chocolate milk, protein shake, chicken & rice.
